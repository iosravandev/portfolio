S

Scrollary

Earn your screen time by learning vocabulary.

Scrollary blocks the apps you choose, such as Instagram or TikTok, using Apple's Screen Time. To unlock them, you scroll a TikTok-style feed of vocabulary cards. Each correct answer earns minutes of screen time. Words you miss go into My Words, where a Recovery Quiz pays double.

🛡

Screen Time integration

Blocks the chosen apps with FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity, with custom Shield UI and Shield Action extensions.

🃏

Endless learning feed

Swipeable word cards with meaning-match and fill-in-the-blank quizzes, audio and example sentences.

⏱

Time economy

Correct answers bank minutes. Boost and Recovery Quizzes (2× rewards) make reviewing worthwhile.

🔄

Offline-first sync

The word catalogue is cached with SwiftData and kept current by delta sync against a NestJS REST API.

📊

Progress & stats

Words learned, points, daily goals and milestones, all calculated on the device.

💳

Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly plans with a free trial through RevenueCat. No account or login needed.

MedCall

Your medication calls you, so you never miss a dose.

Most pill reminders are notifications that get swiped away. MedCall rings like an incoming phone call: a full-screen alarm that sounds even in Silent mode, where you swipe to confirm you took the dose. Live Activities and widgets show your next dose at a glance.

📞

Call-style alarms

Full-screen, ringing reminders built on AlarmKit, with Take, Reject and Snooze actions.

🏝

Live Activities

A live countdown to the next dose on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

🧩

Widgets & controls

Next-dose and daily-progress widgets, with interactive actions through App Intents.

🗓

Flexible schedules

Several doses per day, chosen weekdays, dosage amounts, notes, and pausing/resuming medicines.

🔥

History & streaks

A per-medicine log of taken and missed doses, with streak tracking.

🏗

Clean architecture

MVVM with use cases, repositories and a dependency container on top of Core Data.
